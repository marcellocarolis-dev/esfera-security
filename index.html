UrbanVision AI Platform

From Reactive Monitoring to Intelligent Management

UrbanVision AI is our Enterprise SaaS platform integrating VMS, GIS, and real-time AI. Designed to eliminate operational blind spots and provide cryptographically verifiable security.

Edge Computing & Zero-Trust
Core Architecture

Edge Computing & Zero-Trust Security

Unlike traditional systems, UrbanVision AI processes video locally via Edge Nodes, saving 100% of internet bandwidth. The cloud receives only alerts and metadata via an encrypted, one-way connection—no firewall ports required.

  • Local AI processing (Zero bandwidth waste)
  • Zero-Trust encrypted connection
  • Real-time AI Detections (PPE, Fire, Loitering)
  • Intelligent Wall View (Reduces operator fatigue)

Cryptographic Evidence (SHA-256)

Every AI detection generates a hash, creating a court-defensible digital chain of custody. Prove exactly what was detected, when, and with what confidence—immune to silent edits.

Deterministic Risk Engine

Audit-ready risk scores based on ISO 31000 and AS 4806. Reproducible formulas calculate Probability vs. Consequence, ensuring transparent and verifiable compliance.

Paperless Field Service

Technicians install and verify equipment directly via mobile with granular role-based access. No more Excel spreadsheets—status updates sync in real-time to the central platform.

GIS & Smart Mapping

Integrates Geographic Information Systems to provide a dynamic heat map of risk, allowing proactive resource allocation based on historical events and behavior patterns.
